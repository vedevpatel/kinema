Harm table

5 seeds / 50 eval episodes / 20k steps - indicative ordering evidence, not claim-grade. Each injected defect is trained against a clean arm under the same seed; deltas are defect minus clean. Health-score weights are derived from this table where measured (protocol, weights, leaderboard).

Policy: diffusion (lerobot==0.5.1 examples/training/train_policy.py (diffusion, pusht)), batch 32. Dataset: lerobot/pusht@7628202a2180, 120 train / 24 held-out episodes (kinema load_dataset(max_episodes=144): ids spread evenly over the dataset; first 120 train, last 24 held out (harm_study.py split)). Seeds [0, 1, 2, 3, 4]. Generated 2026-11-16; 11.424 GPU-hours on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB, NVIDIA L4.

Rollout success stayed at the floor for every arm (0-8% at 20k steps, 50 rollouts each), so success deltas cannot separate defects in this protocol - held-out MSE is the outcome metric. v1.2 added action_saturation_30pct (+1183.2% mean, the #2 measured defect behind scale mismatch) and dropped_frames_10pct_25pct (measured benign on pusht/diffusion at 20k steps, +3.3% mean; the dropped-frames check stays WARN because frame gaps still signal a broken recording pipeline). v2 adds two arms and extends duplicates_20pct to 5 seeds (+1.5% mean; the benign-at-20k conclusion stands). Provenance correction: all v2 training ran on L4 (an earlier A100 assumption was wrong; the only A100 runs in this table are the v1 sweep, including the clean seeds 0-2 baselines the v2 run reused). Every run records its accelerator in runs[].gpu. v2 deltas are same-GPU: each arm seed's held-out MSE is compared against the L4 clean baseline (seeds 3,4,5). outlier_burst_5pct: +120.178% mean over 3 seeds - a 6-frame +8-std action burst in 5% of episodes is badly harmful, and the outlier-episodes check fires on exactly this pattern. stale_stats_offset_0.5sigma: +23.73% mean over 3 seeds - normalization stats computed from rows shifted +0.5 std while the training rows stay clean.
